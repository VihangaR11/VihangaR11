## About Me

I am **Vihanga Rathnayake**, a final-year **BComp (Hons) Information Systems** undergraduate at the **University of Sri Jayewardenepura**, while also pursuing **Software Engineering** at the **Open University of Sri Lanka**.

My strength lies at the intersection of **business and technology**. I analyse processes, uncover requirements, model better workflows, design practical systems, and collaborate on their technical implementation. My experience spans **digital government, case management, geotechnical engineering, healthcare, enterprise systems, AI/ML, databases, web applications, and mobile solutions**.

I currently contribute to a **Digital Case Flow Transformation** initiative at Sri Lanka's National Authority for the Protection of Victims of Crime and Witnesses. My long-term direction is toward **Business Analysis, Systems Analysis, Functional Consulting, Solution Consulting, and Digital Transformation** roles.

## Current Focus

- Translating stakeholder needs into **functional, non-functional, security, and data requirements**
- Modelling **As-Is and To-Be processes**, gaps, use cases, roles, and system interactions
- Designing a secure **digital complaint and case-management solution** for a public-sector environment
- Developing an extensible **ML-driven geotechnical report-generation platform**
- Strengthening expertise in **enterprise systems, solution design, and technology-enabled process improvement**

## Featured Work

### Digital Case Flow Transformation — NAPVCW

Public-sector digital transformation initiative focused on improving complaint and case-management workflows through business analysis, process redesign, requirements engineering, security analysis, architecture, and prototype development.

**Core contributions:** stakeholder analysis · As-Is/To-Be processes · gap analysis · BPMN · use cases · FR/NFR · security requirements · role matrix · data dictionary · solution architecture · prototype design

[View repository](https://github.com/VihangaR11/NAPVCW)

### Extensible ML-Driven Geotechnical Report Generation Platform

Final-year research project that transforms Low Strain Pile Integrity Test data into explainable, ASTM D5882-aligned engineering reports. The platform combines document parsing, waveform extraction, signal-feature engineering, hybrid rule-based and ML classification, REST APIs, and automated PDF/Word report generation.

**Technology:** Python · FastAPI · scikit-learn · TensorFlow · OpenCV · pandas · React · PostgreSQL · ReportLab · python-docx

### PileTeST Corporate Web Platform

A production-oriented corporate website migrated to an Astro and TypeScript architecture with reusable components, responsive design, SEO, build validation, performance budgets, automated deployment, and scheduled production health checks.

[View repository](https://github.com/VihangaR11/piletest-web)

### Smart Student Hub

A responsive student-life platform combining study planning, GPA calculation, Pomodoro tools, budgeting, wellbeing support, community features, theming, and browser-based persistence.

[View repository](https://github.com/VihangaR11/smart-campus-hub)
